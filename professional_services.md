---

Assessment of land suitability, water availability, infrastructure, logistics, climate conditions, and operational constraints.

---

## Environmental Feasibility

Review of environmental considerations, sustainability measures, resource efficiency, and ecological impacts.

---

## Operational Feasibility

Assessment of manpower requirements, operational workflows, maintenance needs, and long-term project management.
