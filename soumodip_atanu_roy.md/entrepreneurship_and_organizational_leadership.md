# Founder of Mul Biotech Farms

Founded in **2019**, Mul Biotech Farms began as a biotechnology and sustainable agriculture initiative focused on scientific farming solutions.

Under his leadership, the organization expanded into multiple technical domains including:

- Biotechnology
- Regenerative agriculture
- Environmental consulting
- Climate technology
- Controlled environment agriculture
- Carbon project development
- GIS and MRV systems
- Digital agriculture
- Research and innovation

The incorporation of **Mul Biotech Farms Private Limited in 2026** marked the transition to a structured corporate entity capable of delivering large-scale technical and institutional projects.

# Founder of SequestraBionix Foundation

Recognizing the need for a dedicated public-interest platform, Soumodip Atanu Roy established **SequestraBionix Foundation** to support environmental awareness, scientific outreach, volunteer engagement, and sustainability initiatives.

The Foundation complements the technical work of Mul Biotech Farms by promoting education, community participation, and environmental stewardship.
