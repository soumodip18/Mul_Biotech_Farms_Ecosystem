# Professional Journey

## Executive Summary

Soumodip Atanu Roy is an Indian biotechnology professional, entrepreneur, and climate-tech practitioner whose career integrates scientific research, environmental biotechnology, regenerative agriculture, wastewater management, digital agriculture, climate innovation, and sustainable development. His professional journey reflects the transition from laboratory-based biotechnology research to the development of integrated environmental solutions through entrepreneurship, scientific innovation, and institutional collaboration.

His academic work established a strong foundation in microbial biotechnology, enzyme technology, industrial wastewater treatment, and biological process engineering. Building upon these scientific foundations, he founded Mul Biotech Farms in 2019 with the objective of translating research into practical technologies for agriculture, environmental management, and climate resilience.

Over the following years, his work expanded beyond consultancy into the development of climate-tech solutions integrating biotechnology, regenerative agriculture, GIS, digital monitoring systems, carbon project development, environmental consulting, and artificial intelligence. This evolution culminated in the incorporation of Mul Biotech Farms Private Limited in 2026, establishing a structured organization capable of delivering scientific and technical solutions for government agencies, institutions, industries, and farming communities.

Alongside the commercial enterprise, he established SequestraBionix Foundation to support public-facing environmental initiatives, scientific outreach, volunteer engagement, and sustainability education. Together, these organizations operate as complementary components of the broader Mul Biotech Farms Ecosystem.

His work continues to focus on converting scientific research into scalable environmental technologies that contribute to sustainable agriculture, circular bioeconomy, climate mitigation, ecosystem restoration, and evidence-based decision making.

## Professional Evolution

His career has progressed through five distinct stages:

1. Establishing a scientific foundation through biotechnology education and laboratory research.
2. Advancing into environmental biotechnology and industrial wastewater treatment during postgraduate studies.
3. Founding Mul Biotech Farms as a biotechnology and agricultural consultancy in 2019.
4. Expanding into integrated climate-tech, regenerative agriculture, and digital environmental solutions.
5. Building the Mul Biotech Farms Ecosystem through complementary commercial and nonprofit organizations focused on scientific innovation, environmental sustainability, and long-term societal impact.
