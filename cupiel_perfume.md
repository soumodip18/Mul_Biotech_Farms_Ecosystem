# Cupiel Perfumes

## Overview

**Cupiel Perfumes** is the consumer lifestyle and fragrance brand within the **Mul Biotech Ecosystem**, dedicated to creating premium fragrances and personal care products that combine quality, craftsmanship, and thoughtful design.

Operating as an independent consumer brand, Cupiel focuses on delivering distinctive fragrance experiences while embracing responsible innovation, product excellence, and sustainable business practices where appropriate.

The brand represents the ecosystem's consumer products vertical and complements the broader vision of building organizations that create long-term value across diverse industries.
