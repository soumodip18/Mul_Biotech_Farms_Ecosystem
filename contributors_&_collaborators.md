The ecosystem actively collaborates with students, interns, volunteers, and early-career professionals through structured internship and volunteer programmes.

Participants contribute across multiple domains, including:

- Biotechnology
- Climate Technology

- Regenerative Agriculture
- GIS & Remote Sensing
- Digital MRV
- Data Analytics & Business Intelligence
- Research & Documentation
- Digital Communications

Current and historical internship records are maintained separately through official internship documentation.

# Government Engagements

Mul Biotech Farms collaborates with government departments, public institutions, and statutory bodies through officially designated **Points of Contact (POCs)**, nodal officers, technical committees, and departmental representatives assigned to specific projects and programmes.

Engagements may include:

- Technical Consultations
- Detailed Project Reports (DPRs)
- Pilot Projects
- Capacity Building
- Research Collaborations
- District-Level Programmes
- Climate & Environmental Initiatives
- Monitoring, Reporting & Evaluation
- Technical Advisory Services

Government POCs are assigned on a project-specific basis and may change over time. Individual names are generally not listed unless publicly acknowledged through official documentation.

# Behind the Ecosystem

The Mul Biotech Farms Ecosystem is founded on the belief that meaningful innovation requires interdisciplinary collaboration.

Scientists, engineers, researchers, educators, entrepreneurs, academic institutions, government stakeholders, interns, volunteers, and strategic advisors collectively contribute to the ecosystem's continued evolution.

As the ecosystem grows, new organizations, independent brands, research programmes, and strategic partnerships may emerge while remaining connected through a shared foundation of science, sustainability, and responsible innovation.

> **"One Ecosystem. Many Disciplines. Shared Purpose."**
